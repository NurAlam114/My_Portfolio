Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NurAlamSpace ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our Facebook Page, including commenting on our posts and receiving automated replies via Facebook Messenger.

1. Information We Collect

When you comment on one of our Facebook posts using a specific keyword (e.g. "PDF") to request a resource, we may receive and process the following information via Facebook's Graph API and Webhooks:

  • Your public Facebook name and profile ID (as provided by Facebook for the comment/message event)
  • The text content of your public comment
  • The Page post ID and comment ID associated with your comment
  • Basic message metadata required to send you a reply through Messenger (e.g. timestamps)

We do not collect your password, private messages unrelated to this interaction, payment information, or any data beyond what Facebook shares with us for the purpose of responding to your comment.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To detect when someone requests a resource (such as a PDF) by commenting on our Page
  • To send you the requested resource or a reply via Facebook Messenger
  • To improve and troubleshoot our automated response system

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Comment and message data used to trigger and deliver automated replies is processed through our workflow automation system. We retain only what is necessary to operate this service and do not keep this data longer than needed for troubleshooting and record-keeping purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

This service uses Meta's (Facebook) Graph API and Webhooks to receive comment events and send Messenger replies, and may use workflow automation tools (such as n8n) hosted on our infrastructure to process these events. These providers only receive the minimum data required to perform their function.

5. Your Rights

You may stop this interaction at any time by not commenting with the trigger keyword, blocking our Page on Messenger, or contacting us to request that any data associated with your interaction be deleted.

6. Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
